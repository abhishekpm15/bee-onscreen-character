How it works

Three steps and your bee is carrying messages.

  1. Turn on the beeInstall Buzzy, switch on “Show bee” and save your name.
  2. Swap bee codesYou get a permanent bee code. Share it only with close ones and add theirs.
  3. Start buzzingOnce you've both added each other, hover over the bee, type and press Enter.

Private by design

Buzzy keeps as little as possible. Read the full privacy policy.

  • No accounts, email or phone number needed
  • Messages are never saved on our server
  • Doesn't read the pages you visit or your history
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics
  • Strangers can't message you — only friends you've added
  • Your settings stay in your own browser
